About Aquoral

What is Aquoral?

Aquoral is a protective oral spray that relieves the symptoms of dry mouth. It forms a lipid film that lubricates the mouth and holds moisture in.

What is OGT?

OGT stands for Oxidized Glycerol Triesters, the active ingredient in Aquoral. In simple terms, OGT is oxygenated corn oil. Along with creating a protective layer that helps trap moisture in the oral mucosa, OGT has been shown to promote the good bacteria that help fight gingivitis, a risk factor for heart disease.

What are the ingredients?

Oxidized glycerol triesters (OGT), silicon dioxide, aspartame, and artificial flavoring. See the package for the full ingredient list. Aquoral contains aspartame and should not be used by people with phenylketonuria (PKU).

Is it gluten free?

Yes. Aquoral is a non-toxic, gluten-free, non-controlled substance.

What is the pH of Aquoral?

Aquoral has a neutral pH of 7, similar to water. Many other dry mouth products are acidic with a pH in the 4-6 range. Understanding the acidity of a product and the effects that acidity has on the oral microbiome is important. An unbalanced oral microbiome, dry mouth notwithstanding, can increase the chances of tooth decay and dental caries.

Is there clinical data to support the use of Aquoral?

Yes. Aquoral’s OGT technology has been studied in clinical trials and laboratory tests. See the evidence.

Using Aquoral

How do I use it?

Shake gently, prime the pump before first use, then spray inside your cheeks or onto your tongue and spread it around your mouth with your tongue. Full directions.

How often can I use it?

One dose (2 sprays) into the mouth 3 to 4 times a day.

How long does relief last?

Up to six hours from a single use for many people.

Can I use it at night?

Yes. Many people spray before bed, especially if they use CPAP or breathe through their mouth.

Can I use it with dentures?

Yes. A lubricated mouth can make dentures more comfortable.

Important safety information

Aquoral relieves the symptoms of dry mouth. It does not treat the underlying cause. Read and follow the directions on the package. Do not use if you are allergic to any of its ingredients. Stop use and talk to a health professional if irritation occurs or symptoms persist. Keep out of reach of children.
