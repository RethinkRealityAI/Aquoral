How to use

Two sprays. That’s it.

Aquoral is quick and discreet to use, with no water, no mixing and no rinsing.

  1. 1

    Remove the cap and shake gently

    Hold the canister upright.

  2. 2

    Prime before first use

    Press the pump a few times, away from your face, until a fine mist appears. The pump may need priming again if you haven’t used it for a while.

  3. 3

    Spray twice

    Aim inside one cheek and press once, then the other cheek, or spray onto your tongue.

  4. 4

    Spread it around

    Move your tongue around your mouth to spread the film over your gums, cheeks and palate. Don’t rinse.

How often

3–4 times a day

Or whenever your mouth feels dry. Good moments: when you wake up, before meals, before speaking for a long time, and at bedtime.

Each use
2 sprays
Relief
4 to 6 h

Tips

Get the most from Aquoral

Keep one by the bed

With two canisters per box, keep one at home and one in your bag.

Before meals

Spraying before you eat makes chewing and swallowing easier.

Look after your teeth

Dry mouth raises the risk of cavities. Brush with fluoride toothpaste and see your dentist regularly.

Storage

Store at room temperature, cap on, away from direct heat. Do not use after the expiry date.

Important safety information

Aquoral relieves the symptoms of dry mouth. It does not treat the underlying cause. Read and follow the directions on the package. Do not use if you are allergic to any of its ingredients. Stop use and talk to a health professional if irritation occurs or symptoms persist. Keep out of reach of children.
