Who it helps

Sjögren’s disease

Dry mouth is one of the most common and disruptive symptoms of Sjögren’s. Aquoral can be an important addition to your strategy.

As someone familiar with Sjögren’s Disease, you know that Dry Mouth is a common and disruptive symptom of this inflammatory condition. Patients report significant challenges, including their teeth catching on their cheeks, their tongue catching on their molars, teeth grinding, and drinking a gallon of water or more to keep their mouths lubricated resulting in frequent bathroom trips. “Saliva substitutes” are often used as part of an overall strategy to relieve Sjögren’s Disease-related Dry Mouth.

Aquoral is different and can be an important addition to your dry mouth strategy.

  • Unlike most over-the-counter water-based products, Aquoral’s lipid-based formulation lubricates and creates a protective barrier helping trap moisture in the oral mucosa and helping prevent oral problems such as mucositis
  • Aquoral can be used alone as a single agent or in combination as a cost-effective way to “lock in” the moisturizing effects and prolong the benefit of other dry mouth treatments
  • Aquoral is available in a small lightweight spray that can be easily carried during daily activities

Important safety information

Aquoral relieves the symptoms of dry mouth. It does not treat the underlying cause. Read and follow the directions on the package. Do not use if you are allergic to any of its ingredients. Stop use and talk to a health professional if irritation occurs or symptoms persist. Keep out of reach of children.
