Who it helps

Cannabis

Cannabis use can reduce saliva and cause “cottonmouth”. Here is why it happens and what helps.

Marijuana use can lead to a reduction in saliva production resulting in Dry Mouth, sometimes referred to as “Cottonmouth”. This decrease occurs due to substances in the cannabis plant called cannabinoids, the most common being THC and CBD. These cannabinoids bind to the part of the brain that controls the salivary glands causing a decrease in saliva production and the feeling of “cottonmouth” or widespread oral dryness. This is problematic since saliva is the natural fluid cleanser of oral health; it washes away bacteria and protects the teeth. If your mouth is continuously depleted of saliva, you have less protection against tooth decay and gum disease.

Dry Mouth side effects can range from annoying to debilitating, often impacting the ability to chew, taste, swallow, and speak. Aquoral creates a protective oral shield and lubricates the mucosa, providing relief for 4 to 6 hours.

Remember to drink fluids and avoid sweet substances as general rules of thumb in addition to other good oral hygiene practices. Most importantly, don’t neglect regular dental visits.
