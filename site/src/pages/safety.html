Directions for use

One dose is two sprays.

Up to 4 times a day, as needed.

  1. 1

    Shake gently

    Shake the canister gently.

  2. 2

    Prime the pump

    Prime the pump before first use if needed.

  3. 3

    Spray one dose

    Spray one dose (2 sprays) into the mouth, up to 4 times a day (as needed).

  4. 4

    Spread with your tongue

    Spread the product over dry or inflamed areas of the mouth with the tongue.

Contraindications

Aquoral is contraindicated for anyone with a known history of hypersensitivity to any of its ingredients. Do not use if allergic to any ingredient.

Contains aspartame (a source of phenylalanine): not for people with phenylketonuria (PKU).

Interactions

There are no known (TGO) interactions with medicinal or other products.

{{icon:droplets}}

Actions

Aquoral® is a lipid-based solution resembling human saliva designed to moisten and lubricate the oral cavity, including the oral mucosa of the mouth, tongue and throat, by formation of a lipid film which limits loss of water and restores the viscoelasticity of the oral mucosa. Aquoral also provides protective action against further inflammation of the oral mucosa. Xerostomia (dry mouth) has harmful effects on the oral cavity and quality of life: consequently, management of dry mouth is primarily based on relief of symptoms.

{{icon:check-circle}}

Indications

Aquoral is intended to provide relief from chronic and temporary xerostomia (dry mouth), which may be a result of disease such as Sjögren’s, oral inflammation, medication, chemo or radiotherapy, stress or aging. Aquoral relieves symptoms of dry mouth such as difficulties in swallowing, speech, and changes in taste.

Storage

Store between 20°C and 25°C.

That is 68°F to 77°F. The spray is flammable. Keep it out of reach of children.
