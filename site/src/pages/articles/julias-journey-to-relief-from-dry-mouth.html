Julia’s journey to relief from dry mouth

How Julia, a professional speaker living with Sjögren’s disease, found relief from dry mouth with Aquoral.

Julia’s struggle with Sjögren’s disease began long before her formal diagnosis. She recalls having symptoms as early as 16 years old, such as dry mouth and dry eyes. This autoimmune disorder, which affects the whole body, posed significant challenges for Julia. With dry mouth, or xerostomia, being one of the more notable symptoms, Julia faced pain and complications daily. The sensation of blood in her mouth and throat, teeth catching on her cheeks, and lips splitting from the inside were just a few of the symptoms she struggled with every day.

Discomfort and challenges

As a professional speaker, Julia’s livelihood depends on her ability to speak clearly and communicate effectively. Her dry mouth symptoms made this daunting. To cope with the dryness, Julia had to drink large amounts of water, which only gave temporary relief and led to frequent bathroom trips throughout her day. Nights were just as difficult, as Julia would wake up multiple times to moisten her dry mouth or visit the bathroom. These challenges affected her quality of life, undermined her confidence and made it harder to focus on her speaking engagements.

Transformation and emotional resonance

For Julia, the relief provided by Aquoral was immediate. For someone who had lived with Sjögren’s symptoms for decades, the sensation of her mouth feeling normal and moist was transformative. The effect was so striking that Julia said it felt like having her teenage mouth back. Even though she couldn’t physically cry because of her condition, the emotional impact of this newfound comfort was clear.

Discovery and relief

Using Aquoral also had a dramatic effect on Julia’s quality of life. She said she did not realise a product existed that was so profound, so fast and so long-lasting, allowing her to get on with everyday activities without constantly needing water. As a speaker, she could now perform without discomfort, and she regained her ability to enjoy chewing, tasting and swallowing. Aquoral’s small spray bottle lets Julia use it discreetly in public, a significant improvement over the water-based treatments that required rinsing and only gave short-term relief.

“I don’t even recognize my mouth anymore. Aquoral isn’t liquid gold, it’s liquid normal.”

Julia T., living with Sjögren’s

Looking ahead

Julia’s experience with lipid-based Aquoral Oral Protective Spray was life-changing. She recognised immediately what Aquoral’s unique formulation would mean for her daily life. Julia made it part of her routine, carrying the spray with her and keeping one by her bedside, so she would never be without the relief she had found. In her own words, Aquoral wasn’t “just an improvement; it was a return to normalcy.”

Julia’s journey, from enduring Sjögren’s-related dry mouth to discovering rapid and long-lasting relief with Aquoral, shows how much such advances matter for people living with chronic autoimmune disorders. By providing lubrication and a protective coating, Aquoral Oral Protective Spray addressed both Julia’s physical discomfort and her quality of life, helping her regain a sense of normalcy and comfort she hadn’t felt in years.

Important safety information

Aquoral relieves the symptoms of dry mouth. It does not treat the underlying cause. Read and follow the directions on the package. Do not use if you are allergic to any of its ingredients. Stop use and talk to a health professional if irritation occurs or symptoms persist. Keep out of reach of children.
