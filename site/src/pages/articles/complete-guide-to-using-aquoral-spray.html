What xerostomia is, how Aquoral’s OGT formula works, and how to apply the spray for the best relief.

Xerostomia (dry mouth) negatively affects oral health and overall well-being. It happens when the salivary glands don’t produce enough saliva, causing oral discomfort and other complications. What is xerostomia and how does it affect oral health?

Xerostomia is a constant feeling of dry mouth caused by reduced saliva production. Saliva helps with digestion, protects tooth enamel, and lubricates the mouth. When saliva flow decreases, it can make swallowing, speaking, and tasting difficult and increase the risk of cavities and gum disease.

What are the common causes and symptoms of dry mouth?

Several factors can lead to xerostomia, including:

  • Medications: Many medications, both prescribed and over-the-counter, like antihistamines and antidepressants, can cause dry mouth as a side effect.
  • Chronic conditions: Diseases such as diabetes, Sjögren’s, and Parkinson’s can affect salivary gland function.
  • Lifestyle factors: Habits like smoking or drinking too much alcohol can also reduce saliva production.

Identifying these causes is important to guide proper management and treatment of xerostomia.

How does xerostomia impact saliva production and oral mucosa?

Lower saliva output reduces the mouth’s ability to neutralize acid, which raises the risk of tooth decay. Lack of lubrication causes dry, irritated mouth tissues that are more prone to infection and sores. These effects highlight the need for targeted treatments to relieve dry mouth.
