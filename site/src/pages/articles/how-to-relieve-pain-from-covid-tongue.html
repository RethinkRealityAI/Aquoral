How to relieve pain from COVID tongue

What COVID tongue is, why it happens, and ways to feel better.

The coronavirus, also known as COVID-19, can cause various health issues beyond just problems with breathing. One lesser-known problem is something called “COVID tongue,” where the tongue can feel uncomfortable and painful. In this article, we’ll talk about what COVID tongue is, why it happens, and ways to feel better, including using Aquoral Spray to relieve the associated discomfort.

Understanding COVID tongue

COVID-19 can cause different symptoms, not only affecting the lungs but also causing issues in different parts of the body. COVID tongue is one of these issues, and it happens because of changes to the surface of the tongue that can cause discomfort and pain. COVID tongue joins a list of oral manifestations seen in COVID-19 infections, such as lesions on the mucosa, dysgeusia (distorted sense of taste), and dry mouth.

Why does COVID tongue happen?

We’re still figuring out exactly why COVID tongue happens, but it seems connected to how the virus affects the whole body. The tongue is a sensitive part of the body with lots of blood vessels. When the immune system fights the virus, it can affect the small bumps on the tongue (the papillae), making them disappear, also known as “depapillation”. This not only makes the tongue uncomfortable but also changes how things taste, adding another layer to the challenges faced by COVID-19 patients.

Dietary measures for relief

While there is no specific diet that directly targets COVID tongue, adopting certain dietary measures can contribute to overall oral health and potentially ease discomfort. Here are some recommendations:

  1. Hydration is key: Adequate hydration is crucial for oral health. Drinking plenty of water helps maintain moisture in the mouth, which can be soothing for individuals experiencing dry mouth associated with COVID tongue.
  2. Soft and cool foods: Choose soft and cool foods that are gentle on the tongue. Yogurt, smoothies, and ice cream can provide relief and offer a cooling sensation.
  3. Avoid irritants: Steer clear of spicy, acidic, or hot foods that may make discomfort worse. These can further irritate the tongue and aggravate the symptoms of COVID tongue.

Best practices for managing COVID tongue

In addition to dietary considerations, certain best practices can help in managing COVID tongue:

  1. Gentle oral hygiene: Maintain a gentle oral hygiene routine to avoid further irritation. Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and consider alcohol-free mouthwashes.
  2. Monitor symptoms: Keep a close eye on the progression of symptoms. If there are concerns about the severity of COVID tongue or if it persists, seeking professional medical advice is paramount.
