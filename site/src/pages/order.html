Important safety information

Aquoral relieves the symptoms of dry mouth. It does not treat the underlying cause. Read and follow the directions on the package. Do not use if you are allergic to any of its ingredients. Contains aspartame: not for people with phenylketonuria (PKU). Stop use and talk to a health professional if irritation occurs or symptoms persist. Keep out of reach of children.
